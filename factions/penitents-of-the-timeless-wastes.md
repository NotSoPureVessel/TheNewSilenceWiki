## Penitents of the Timeless Wastes

The term for the most extreme worshipeprs of Theos, Goddess of Time, watcher of stars and fates. They test themselves in the Timeless Wastes. There are two distinct regions of the Timeless Wastes, the Glasstone Walk and Neverdawn Frost.

The Glasstone Walk is a flat plane of perfectly smooth, reflective stone. It constantly rains in the Walk, yet the stone never erodes. It is home to many warlocks, wizards, and sorcerors who wish to perform their arcane studies in an isolated environment. As such, the skyline is dotted by towers.

The Neverdawn Frost is an untamed snow-laden tundra which resets to the previous morning each dawn, reliving a similar day over and over. It is a place where every change made is a ruthless fight against nature. The most disciplined monks of time and those seeking peace or redemption often find their way here. It is additionally home to the Thinking Hare, who answers any one question to those who can pass the Trial of the Monk.

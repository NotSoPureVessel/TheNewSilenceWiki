## Astral Court

Located and largely isolated within the Astral Plane. Bound by a pact of non-interference which largely limits their interaction with the Heartlands. As is the case with all factions in other planes, they cannot be contacted through traditional sending means without a technology known as a "sim card" which anchors the spell across planes similar to how the bags of holding and devouring do.

Worships Theos, Goddess of Time, watcher of stars and fates. Even among the followers of Theos, an incredibly high concentration of her chosen servants and demigods. Penitents who perform exceptional actions in the name of Theos' ideals are sometimes granted a second life after death in the Court. 

There are all manner of beings in the Court which don't exist in the Heartlands, including the Gith, Autognomes, Plasmoids, Aasimar, Thri-Kreen, and Space Hamsters.

Outside the Court, there seem to be other forces at play in the Astral Plane, including arsonist aliens and space pirates.

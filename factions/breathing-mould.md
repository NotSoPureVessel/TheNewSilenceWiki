## The Breathing Moulde

One pillar of the Garnus Aliance.

Worships Theos, Goddess of Time, watcher of stars and fates.

The Birthplace of the Warforged, nestled deep into the heart of the Garnus mountains. Warforged are created and grow up here, before they select their purpose and enter the world. The deeper chambers are inaccessible since the passing of Galliard Timbers, but could still be accessed with his passcode from the peak of the highest mountain in Garnus. Several years ago, production of Warforged stopped entirely for unknown reasons.
